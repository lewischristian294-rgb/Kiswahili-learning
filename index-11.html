Your learning path

Four levels. One step at a time.

Choose a level to load its lesson. Complete the short quiz at the end to mark that lesson as finished on this device.

Level 1 • Starter

Msingi

Greetings, introductions, pronunciation, numbers, simple questions, and basic sentence structure.

Level 2 • Elementary

Maendeleo

Daily routines, present and past actions, shopping, directions, and describing people and places.

Level 3 • Intermediate

Ufasaha

Future plans, opinions, longer conversations, object markers, and connected ideas.

Level 4 • Advanced

Uhodari

Nuance, formal and informal registers, complex clauses, storytelling, and discussion.
